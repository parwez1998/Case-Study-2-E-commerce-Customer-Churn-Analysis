Business Context, Strategic Objectives & Scope

Framing & Assumptions
Establishing quantifiable benchmarks, analytical formulas, and operational constraints for the retention initiative.
Business Problem & Operational Challenge

Boxify operates a premium curated online subscription-box service in India. While top-of-funnel customer acquisition has scaled rapidly to 5,000 recorded subscribers across 12 metropolitan and Tier-2 markets, revenue growth is severely constrained by an overall 47.08% churn rate.

High Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) combined with early churn prevents the recovery of marketing spend, depressing average churned Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) to ₹4,868 compared to ₹9,082 for active subscribers.

Case Study Business Objectives
  • Primary Objective: Reduce overall customer churn rate by 5% to 10% (relative) within a 6-month operational horizon.
  • Target Retention: Preserve between 118 and 235 at-risk subscribers.
  • Revenue Protection: Recover an estimated ₹10.7 Lakh to ₹21.3 Lakh in recurring annualized revenue.
  • Predictive Capability: Deploy an in-browser explainable machine learning model to detect high-risk active accounts prior to cancellation.
Standardized KPI & Metric Definitions
Portfolio Churn Rate: Count(Churned) / Total Customers
Tenure (Months): Calendar months elapsed from JoinDate to CancelDate (or snapshot date if active).
Monthly Rate (INR): TotalSpent / max(TenureMonths, 1)
Annualised Lost Revenue: ∑ (MonthlyRate × 12) for all churned subscribers.
Early Churn Hazard: Churned subscribers with TenureMonths < 6.
Analytical Constraints & Scope
  • Observation Window: Exactly 36 operating months (November 2023 through November 2026).
  • Snapshot Cutoff Date: 2026-11-21. All active subscriber tenure is locked to this anchor date.
  • Billing Cycle Assumption: Cancellations are recognized strictly at the completion of monthly billing cycles (no mid-cycle pro-rata refunds).
  • Data Boundary: Exclusively utilizes the verified 5,000-row customer dataset.

Appendix A1: Methodology & Mathematical Formulations

Technical Reference
Rigorous statistical and machine learning foundations implemented in pure, dependency-free JavaScript.
1. Derived Business Metrics Formulations
Tenure (Months): Calculated as exact completed calendar months from JoinDate to CancelDate (or 2026-11-21 if active): 
Tenure = max(0,
  (End.Year − Start.Year) × 12
  + End.Month − Start.Month
  − (End.Day < Start.Day ? 1 : 0)
)
Monthly Run Rate (INR): Derived as TotalSpent / max(TenureMonths, 1).
Annualised Lost Revenue: Expressed as ∑ (MonthlyRate × 12) for all churned accounts.
Monthly Cohort Churn Rate: Cancellations during month M divided by total subscribers active at the beginning of month M: 
Monthly Churn Rate =
  CancelCount(Month M) / ActiveSubscribers(Start of M)
2. In-Browser Logistic Regression Architecture
Binary Classification Formulation: Churn probability P(y=1|X) modeled via standard Sigmoid activation: 
P(y=1|X) = 1 / (1 + exp(-(β0 + ∑ βi · Xi)))
Optimization: Cross-entropy loss minimized via deterministic gradient descent (learning rate α = 0.5, 500 epochs).
Evaluated Features: Normalized Age, Normalized Engagement Score, Subscription Plan (Monthly/Quarterly with Annual reference), Payment Gateway (UPI/Other with Card reference), and Location Tier (Tier 2 vs Metro).
AUC-ROC Calculation: Derived analytically using the Mann-Whitney U test statistic over the independent 20% test holdout set.

Boxify Analytics: Data, Assumptions & Formulations

1. Data Provenance & Snapshots

This analytics deliverable analyzes 5,000 subscriber records from boxify_customers.csv spanning from 23 November 2023 to 21 November 2026. The snapshot anchor date is set to 2026-11-21.

2. Derived Fields & Formulas

  • Churned: Customer records with non-empty CancelDate.
  • Tenure (Months): Calendar months between JoinDate and CancelDate (or 2026-11-21 for active members).
  • Monthly Rate: TotalSpent / max(TenureMonths, 1).
  • Annualised Lost Revenue: ∑ (MonthlyRate × 12) for churned accounts.
  • Early Churn: Churned customers with TenureMonths < 6.
  • Monthly Cohort Churn Rate: Cancellations in month M divided by customers active at start of month M.

3. Analytical Constraints & Business Assumptions

  • All subscriptions renew on a monthly cycle.
  • Cancellations occur strictly at the end of billing cycles (no pro-rata refunds).
  • Currency is Indian Rupee (₹ INR) with standard Lakh/Crore conversions (1 Lakh = 100,000; 1 Crore = 10,000,000).
