Boxify Subscription Box Service · 3-Year Customer Study

Why Are Subscribers Leaving Early — And How Do We Keep Them?

Boxify delivers monthly lifestyle boxes across India. Over the past year, more customers cancelled before finishing 6 months. This deck explains why subscribers leave, who is most at risk, and how we can cut cancellations by 5–10% in the next 6 months.

🎯 Our 3 Main Goals

  • Cut Early Cancellations: Lower customer loss by 5–10% over the next 6 months.
  • Spot High-Risk Groups: Find which plans, cities, and age groups leave most.
  • Build Loyalty Tactics: Design targeted offers to keep subscribers past month 6.

📊 What We Measure

  • Customers Who Left: Share of subscribers who cancelled.
  • Value per Customer & Lost Sales: Total spent before leaving and annualised loss.
  • How Active & Months Stayed: Activity score (0–100) and average months subscribed.

🛡️ Rules & Limits

  • 3 Years of Records: 5,000 customers up to Nov 21, 2026.
  • Monthly Renewals: Cancellations are counted at the end of a billing cycle.
  • Dataset Only: All numbers come strictly from boxify_customers.csv.
